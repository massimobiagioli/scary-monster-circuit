# ADR-0020: Follow from the nearest sensor within 30 cm

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11

## Context

`Follow` used the *nearest* sensor within 60 cm. With the two HC-SR04 modules
reading unevenly (one close, the other far), the prop reacted even when nobody
was in front, and the head kept turning: it "seemed crazy".

## Decision

- `Follow` starts when the **nearest** sensor sees somebody within
  `FOLLOW_DISTANCE_CM` = 30 cm (one sensor is enough), and ends when the nearest
  goes past `FOLLOW_EXIT_CM` = 40 cm (hysteresis).
- `Action` requires **both** sensors within `ACTION_DISTANCE_CM` = 10 cm.
- `Reading::both_within(cm)` is the shared "both eyes see this close" test used
  by `Action`.

## Consequences

- A person seen by a single sensor up close still makes the head follow (for
  example one eye at 5 cm and the other far away), which the strict "both"
  version missed.
- The 30 cm threshold is smaller than the old 60 cm, so a person off to the side
  at a distance is ignored.
- `FOLLOW_DISTANCE_CM`/`FOLLOW_EXIT_CM` are the two numbers to tune if the prop
  should react from farther away.
