# ADR-0002: Two side-mounted distance sensors for tracking

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

## Context

To make the head follow a person, the prop needs to know where the person is,
not only how far away they are. A single sensor cannot tell left from right.

## Decision

- Use **two** distance sensors mounted on the left and right sides of the head.
- Each sensor reports a distance in centimeters; the firmware compares the two
  readings to decide which way to turn.
- The closest reading also drives the state machine thresholds (follow and
  action distances).

## Consequences

- The head can turn towards the nearest side without extra hardware.
- A dead-band is used so small differences do not make the head jitter.
- If one sensor fails, the other still provides a usable distance.
