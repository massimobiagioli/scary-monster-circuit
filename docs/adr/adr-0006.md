# ADR-0006: Breadboard-style pictures for the assembly guide

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

## Context

The step-by-step guide must look like a LEGO booklet: a child of about 10 years
old should be able to build the circuit from the pictures alone. Flowcharts and
"mind map" diagrams do not show where a wire physically goes.

## Decision

- Assembly steps are shown as **top-view breadboard pictures** with the parts
  and the coloured jumper wires.
- The pictures are drawn with Python and Pillow by the reusable tool at
  `tools/breadboard/`, which runs in its own `.venv`.
- One picture is produced per build step; the new part is highlighted in every
  picture and the previous parts stay in place.
- The pictures are generated into `schematics/images/` (git-ignored) and are
  embedded in the step-by-step Markdown, which `make schematics` converts to
  PDF.

## Consequences

- The wiring is shown the way it is actually built, not as an abstract graph.
- `make schematics` first draws the pictures, then builds the PDFs.
- Changing the wiring means updating the drawing tool, not hand-editing images.
